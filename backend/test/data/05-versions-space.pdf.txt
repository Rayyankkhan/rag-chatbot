

Jinnah University for Women (JUW) - 
Knowledge Base Content 
1. University Introduction 
• Founder: Al Haj Moulvi Rayazuddin Ahmed (T.I). 
• Vision: "To educate mother, to educate the nation." 
• Status: First women's university in Pakistan, HEC recognized. 
• Location: Nazimabad, Karachi. 
2. Admission Process & Eligibility (2026-27) 
• Cycles: Spring aur Fall admissions. 
• General Eligibility: * Minimum 50% marks Intermediate (Pre-Medical/Pre-
Engineering/Commerce) mein. 
o 60% marks Software Engineering aur specific professional programs ke liye 
zaroori hain. 
• Entrance Test: University apna test conduct karti hai ya NTS (NAT-I/II) ke results 
accept karti hai. 
• Documents Required: 
o Matriculation Certificate & Marksheet. 
o Intermediate Part-I & II Marksheets. 
o CNIC/B-Form copy. 
o 3 Passport size photographs. 
3. Specific Department: Faculty of Science & Technology 
Ye data chatbot ke liye boht kaam aayega: 
Department of Computer Science & IT 
• Programs: BS (CS), BS (SE), BS (IT), BS (Artificial Intelligence), BS (Cyber Security). 
• Core Subjects (Example BS-SE): 
o Introduction to Computing & Programming. 
o Object-Oriented Programming (OOP). 
o Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA). 
o Database Management Systems (DBMS). 
o Software Requirement Engineering. 
• Electives: Mobile App Development, Deep Learning, Big Data, DevOps. 
Department of Pharmacy (Pharm-D) 
• Duration: 5 Years (10 Semesters). 

• Accreditation: Pharmacy Council of Pakistan se approved. 
4. Fee Structure (Estimated) 
• Application Processing Fee: PKR 3,000/- 
• Admission Fee (One-time): PKR 20,000/- 
• Tuition Fee (Per Credit Hour): Approx. PKR 9,000/- (Note: Fees department ke hisab 
se thori vary kar sakti hai). 
• Scholarships: * Merit-based: Top students ke liye. 
o Need-based: Financial support ki zaroorat wale bacho ke liye. 
5. Campus Facilities 
• Transport: Karachi ke har ilaqe ke liye buses/points available hain. 
• Labs: Dedicated Software Labs, Physics, aur Chemistry Labs. 
• Library: Physical library ke saath digital library access bhi hai.
